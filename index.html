Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to GPS Plot ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our high-precision Android GIS and land measurement application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access or use the application.

2. Information We Collect

GPS Plot is designed with an offline-first architecture. We prioritize local data storage and minimize cloud data collection:

  • Precise Location Data: The app requests runtime location permissions to auto-center the map, compute GPS accuracy metrics (±X.Xm), and accurately record property boundary pins and land measurements. This location data is processed locally on your device in real-time.
  • Saved Parcels & Local Database: Custom-named polygon parcels, coordinates, area metrics, perimeters, and timestamps are securely stored locally on your device using a local Room database. We do not sync or upload your custom property surveys to external servers unless explicitly shared by you via system share intents.
  • Search Queries: When you use the live search autocomplete feature to find locations or addresses, queries are processed through standard geocoding services to center your map camera.

3. Advertising & Monetization

To keep GPS Plot free for surveyors, farmers, and landowners, the app displays advertisements:

  • Interstitial Ads: The app displays automated full-page sponsored interstitial advertisements upon opening and during map usage sessions.
  • Ad Partners: Ad networks may collect anonymous device identifiers, advertising IDs, and interaction data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in compliance with applicable platform guidelines.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain high-precision GIS and land measurement features.
  • Calculate land extents (Acres, Cents, Square Meters, Square Feet) and perimeters accurately.
  • Manage saved local property records and allow easy sharing or re-loading onto the canvas.
  • Display relevant sponsored advertisements to support app maintenance and ongoing development.

5. Data Security & Offline Privacy

Because your custom polygon parcels and measurements are stored in a local database (Room) on your Android device, you retain complete ownership and control over your survey data. We implement reasonable technical measures to secure app performance, though no electronic transmission or digital storage is 100% secure.

6. Professional Legal Disclaimer

GPS measurements, boundary mapping, and area calculations provided by GPS Plot are estimates intended solely for reference, farming, and preliminary planning purposes. They do not replace official cadastral surveys, legal deeds, or certified land surveyor assessments.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
